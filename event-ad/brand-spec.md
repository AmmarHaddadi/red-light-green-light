# Squid Game event advertisement

Standalone local static website. Kinetic editorial invitation for a distant TV audience.
Design calibration: visual variance 7, motion 7, density 4, asset dependence 6, brand fidelity 8.

Logo: assets/Squid-Game-Logo.png (user supplied).
Palette: #ed1b76, #f44786, #ffffff, #249f9c, #037a76; near-black #101313 for contrast.
Typography: local Impact / Arial Narrow for display; Arial / Helvetica for supporting text.
Spacing: viewport-scaled, sharp corners, thin rules, one offset player ticket.
Motion: explanatory marketing loop; game and headline reveals every six seconds; portrait crossfade.
Transform and opacity animations, strong ease-out cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1), 700ms text reveal.
QR and countdown stay stationary. Reduced motion removes translations and decorative motion.
